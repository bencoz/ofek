Privacy policy

Effective 5 November 2026

Ofek keeps your data on your device. It has no accounts, no servers of its own, no analytics and no ads.

Who we are

Ofek is made by Ben Cohen. Questions about this policy: ofektv.app@gmail.com.

What Ofek does not do

  • It has no accounts.
  • It has no developer servers.
  • It has no analytics, ads or tracking.
  • It has no third-party SDKs that collect data.
  • It does not sell or share your data.

What stays on your device

  • The provider login you enter: server address, username and password. It is encrypted with a key in the Android Keystore.
  • Your favourites, pinned categories, recently watched channels and settings.
  • A cached channel list and programme guide from your provider.

What leaves your device, and to whom

Ofek connects to the provider server you enter, to fetch the channel list, the guide, logos and video. It also connects to any other address your provider’s data points to, such as a host for channel logos or a video server. Those hosts see your IP address. Your provider sees your IP address and your login, as with any player. Your provider’s own privacy policy covers what it does with them. The connection may be unencrypted HTTP if that is what your provider uses.

Demo mode lets you try Ofek without a provider. It streams sample videos from GitHub Pages (bencoz.github.io). Its servers see your IP address, like any website you visit.

If you choose Send feedback, your email app opens a message to us with Ofek’s version, your device model and its Android version. We receive that, and your email address, only if you send it.

Local network only

On a TV, while Sign in or Settings → Phone is on screen, Ofek runs a small web page on your home network. You can use it to type your login on a phone. It runs for at most 10 minutes. Each visit needs a one-time code shown on the TV.

Backup and restore, and “copy setup from the TV” to a phone, also use only your local network. They run only when you start them.

Android may ask you for the “local network” permission for this.

Google Play

Google Play may collect crash and performance diagnostics under Google’s privacy policy. Ofek itself adds none.

Keeping and deleting your data

Everything stays until you sign out, which removes your login, or until you clear Ofek’s storage or uninstall it, which removes everything. Nothing else is held anywhere, except emails you choose to send us. Ask, and we will delete those.

Security

Your login is encrypted at rest. The phone page needs a one-time code and closes after 10 minutes.

Children

Ofek is for adults (18 and over). It is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will post the update on this page with a new effective date.
